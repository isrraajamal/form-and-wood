Copenhagen Workshop

Formed by Hand. Grounded in Wood.
Built for Quiet Permanence.

Founded in Bredgade, Copenhagen, FORM & WOOD is an architectural furniture atelier committed to slow craftsmanship, sustainable Scandinavian forestry, and heirloom joinery that withstands generations.

“We design not for the transient novelty of seasons, but for the weight of permanence — creating chairs that mature with age, gathering the warm patina of human lives and quiet morning rituals.”

01 / TIMBER

Kiln-Dried Hardwoods

Every frame is shaped from FSC-certified European ash, white oak, or sustainably harvested plantation teak, dried slowly to 8% internal moisture to prevent seasonal movement.

02 / JOINERY

Blind Mortise & Tenon

Structural timber joints are hand-cut and interlocked with turned timber dowels. We never use hidden screws, brackets, or toxic adhesives to hold structural stress points.

03 / WEAVE

French Cane Webbing

Natural rattan cane peeled into supple strands, soaked in rainwater, and hand-tensioned into breathable octagonal patterns that mold gently to the sitter’s weight over time.

04 / PURITY

Cold-Pressed Oils

Our timber surfaces are sealed exclusively with solvent-free Danish linseed oil and virgin beeswax, allowing the wood to breathe and breathe back organic warmth into your home.

Christian Møller • Head of Atelier
The Makers

Human touch in an era of mechanical speed.

In our Copenhagen workshop, each piece passes through the hands of just two master craftsmen: one joiner who shapes and planes the timber architecture, and one upholsterer who stitches the virgin wool or tensions the French cane.

Every completed specimen is branded with the atelier's brass registry plate, identifying its batch serial number, the artisan's signature, and the harvest coordinates of the timber.

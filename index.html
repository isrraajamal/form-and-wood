[ 02 — our philosophy ]

furniture that listens
before it speaks.

Most modern furniture is either too stiff to relax in or too generic to admire. We operate in the curious middle ground: sculptural, architectural pieces hand-tailored with obsessively plush ergonomics and heirloom joinery.

01 //

organic architecture

Solid European timber frames joined by traditional mortise and tenon, counterbalanced with tactile, fluid curves.

02 //

tactile honesty

Heavyweight bouclé, virgin wools from ethical mills, and sustainable natural foams that conform to your posture.

03 //

lifetime repairability

Every upholstery skin is fully detachable and serviceable for decades of daily companionship.

Mortise & Tenon Joinery Solid teak frames locked by hand-cut joints, no screws, built to last generations.
Hand-Woven Natural Cane Each strand is soaked, woven and tensioned by hand for a breathable, lasting seat.

[ 05 — concierge & guidance ]

frequently asked
questions.

Clear, transparent guidance on White-Glove delivery, material swatches, bespoke commissions, and warranty coverage.

Our two-person specialist freight crew delivers directly into your room of choice across Europe and the UK. They unbox the piece, level the legs, inspect the finish, and remove 100% of the eco-friendly protective packaging materials.

Yes. We offer free material swatch folios containing physical specimens of our A-grade plantation teak, smoked dark walnut, Scandinavian white oak, textured linen, and heavyweight virgin bouclé. Swatch kits dispatch within 24 hours via courier.

All structural mortise-and-tenon joints, kiln-dried timber frames, structural leg anchors, and cane webbing tension are covered against defects or loosening for a full decade. If any structural failure occurs, we repair or replace the piece at zero charge.

Pieces in our active drop (like Batch #04) are in stock at our Copenhagen warehouse and dispatch within 48 to 72 hours. Bespoke atelier commissions tailored to custom room dimensions require 4 to 6 weeks of dedicated handcraft.

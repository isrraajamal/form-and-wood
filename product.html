Nordic Sculptural Bouclé Lounge

★★★★★ 4.9 · 38 atelier collector reviews

Monolithic architectural armchair engineered for slow, intimate spaces. Crafted with hand-chiseled mortise-and-tenon interlocking timber joints, high-resilience natural latex ergonomic core, and dense virgin tactile bouclé upholstery.

Upholstery Fabric Virgin Sage Bouclé
Timber Frame Architecture Kiln-Dried European Ash
10-Year Joinery Warranty — Hand-chiseled mortise-and-tenon architecture.
Complimentary White-Glove Placement across Europe in room of choice.
Dispatches in 48h — Secured in crated organic carton packaging.
88cm Width × 82cm Depth × 76cm Total Height.
Seat Height: 42cm • Armrest Clearance: 64cm • Weight: 24.5 kg.
Engineered for upright ergonomic posture with deep lumbar relaxation angle.
Sourced from sustainably managed, FSC-certified Scandinavian forests. Kiln-dried to 8% moisture equilibrium to eliminate seasonal warping. All structural corners feature blind through-mortise joinery locked by hand-turned wooden dowels — zero plastic connectors or disposable screws.
Delivered by a dedicated two-person furniture specialist team. They will carry the armchair into your room of choice, position it per your request, inspect all finishes, and remove all 100% recyclable crating materials.
Sealed with solvent-free Danish cold-pressed linseed oil and natural beeswax. Wipe with a dry, lint-free cloth. Complimentary maintenance wax tin included with every atelier drop.
